“We refuse neon frivolity. We return to blood crimson, ancient evergreen, and the enduring resilience of the handloom.”

Streetwear in the subcontinent was never meant to be a derivative photocopy of Western skate culture. Streetwear here was born in the bazaars, the mill districts of Ahmedabad and Kanpur, the weavers’ colonies of Varanasi, and the defiance of everyday folks claiming their space on packed city avenues.

STANDARDS
01 / WEIGHT & HONESTY

Heavyweight Integrity

We do not manufacture disposable featherweight clothing. Every garment carries tactile weight—ranging from 360 GSM to 540 GSM—designed to withstand decades of friction, movement, and atmospheric wear.

02 / SUBCONTINENTAL DRAPE

Anatomical Sovereignty

Western tailoring constricts; subcontinental clothing drapes. We design silhouettes that allow air to circulate between textile and skin, using pleated volume and fallen shoulders.

03 / LIVING COLORS

Earthen Patina

Our dyes are alive. Over years of wear, the deep crimsons, bronzes, and evergreens develop an individual patina shaped by your city’s climate, sunshine, and wash cycles.
